# Monster Hunter World: Iceborne icons and images

- `icons/`: equipment icons by rarity, Monster Hunter Wiki `MHWI-<Kind>_Icon_Rare_<N>`.
- `decorations/`, `slots/`: Monster Hunter Wiki `MHWI-Decoration_Level_*` (slots tinted gold).
- `weapons/`: Monster Hunter Wiki weapon renders (`MHWI-<Name>_Render`).
- `sets/`: Monster Hunter Wiki armor set renders (`MHWI-<Set>_Armor_Male_Render`).

Capcom artwork, used locally like the other icons. Built by `tool/data/fetch_world_icons.py`.
