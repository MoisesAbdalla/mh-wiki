# Monster Hunter Wilds icons and images

- `icons/`: equipment icons by rarity, Monster Hunter Wiki `MHWA-<Kind>_Icon_Rare_<N>`.
- `decorations/`, `slots/`: Monster Hunter Wiki `MHWilds-Decoration_Level_*` (slots tinted gold).
- `weapons/`: Monster Hunter Wiki weapon renders (Module:MHWilds/data/Weapons/RendersDict.json).
- `armor/`: Kiranico Wilds armor thumbnails (male model).

Capcom artwork, used locally like the other icons. Built by `tool/data/fetch_wilds_icons.py`.
